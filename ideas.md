# INTEVIA Website — Design Direction

## Ground-truth reference specification

The supplied MNTN reference establishes the structural and editorial direction: a deep midnight page field, immersive opening scene, restrained navigation, large serif-led statements, oversized service numerals, alternating text-and-visual story blocks, and a dense but calm dark footer. This site will not reuse the hiking subject matter or copy the reference; it will translate the rhythm into INTEVIA's business-transformation narrative.

## Chosen approach — **The Strategic Ascent**

### Design Movement

Dark editorial consulting, influenced by premium annual reports and contemporary Swiss information design. It combines the cinematic vertical progression of the supplied reference with precise information hierarchy appropriate for executive decision-makers.

### Core Principles

1. **Clarity through contrast:** Ultra-dark navy creates calm focus; sharply limited electric blue identifies the moments of intelligence and action.
2. **A narrative, not a brochure:** Content develops from uncertainty to actionable execution as visitors scroll.
3. **Editorial asymmetry:** Alternating image panels, offset content columns, and monumental service numbers avoid a generic centred-card landing page.
4. **Measured confidence:** Direct, useful language and controlled visual motion communicate rigorous business practice.

### Color Philosophy

The brand's midnight navy `#0D1B2A` is the primary environment, reflecting depth, discretion, and analytical judgement. Electric INTEVIA blue `#1976FF` is reserved as a decisive action signal, inspired by the V in the supplied mark. Steel `#6B7280`, soft grey `#D1D5DB`, and off-white `#F5F7FA` provide a disciplined tonal scale for reading and separation.

### Layout Paradigm

The page is a vertical transformation path. A full-bleed hero establishes the business challenge, then each service appears as a numbered editorial chapter in an alternating two-column composition. The methodology becomes a horizontal decision pipeline, and the diagnostic entry service breaks the flow with a luminous blue intervention panel.

### Signature Elements

1. Oversized low-contrast chapter numerals that sit behind service copy.
2. The **blue V aperture**: angled blue facets that echo the brand mark at dividers, badges, and CTAs.
3. Fine silver guideline rules and compact uppercase service labels that lend analytical precision.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions answer the visitor's intent without spectacle. Navigation scrolls directly to each relevant section. Service panels reveal a tighter blue rule and arrow on hover; calls-to-action have a tactile press response.

### Animation

On first appearance, sections enter with a 240ms opacity-and-translate transition, staggered gently by service chapter. The V aperture uses a low-key blue sheen; no continuous distracting motion. All animation is disabled for reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System

**DM Serif Display** carries the major English editorial statements, creating authority and strategic scale. **Manrope** carries navigation, Thai supporting copy, captions, and service detail for clarity across scripts. Headings use dramatic size contrast; labels are uppercase, tracked, and compact.

### Brand Essence

**INTEVIA transforms business insight into product and operational action for organisations ready to build with intent.**

Personality: **analytical, decisive, grounded.**

### Brand Voice

Headlines are clear, directional, and outcome-led. CTAs sound like an invitation to make a decision rather than a generic sales prompt.

> "Build what matters. Improve what moves the business."

> "Start with a diagnostic, then act with confidence."

### Wordmark & Logo

Use the supplied INTEVIA wordmark concept in a clean high-contrast lockup. The interface also uses a standalone **IV / V-aperture** mark as an icon and favicon, rendered at a clearly visible scale.

**INTEVIA Signal Blue — `#1976FF`**
